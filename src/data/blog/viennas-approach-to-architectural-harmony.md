Vienna manages its architectural identity through a philosophy often called _the dialogue of eras_. Rather than trying to make new buildings look old, the city encourages a sharp, visible contrast that respects the scale and _rhythm_ of the historic surroundings.

## The _Infill_ Strategy

Vienna is famous for its _infill_ architecture, where a modern building is slotted into a gap in a row of historic houses. These gaps, were previously historic buildings before they are being destroyed or demolished, where new architecture is implemented, acts as a _bridge_ between new architecture and the historical presence.

## Adaptive Reuse of Historic Buildings

The city excels at taking massive historic shells and gutting them for modern use. Take the _MuseumsQuartier_ as an example, once the imperial stables, this complex now houses modern art museums. The contrast between the yellow Baroque stables and the minimalist, basalt blocks of the museums is a masterclass in visual tension.

## Distinct Modern Districts

To preserve the skyline of the historic center, the city directs high-rise modern developments to specific zones across the Danube River, created a _mini-Manhattan_, which houses many of the tallest skyscrapers in Austria.
